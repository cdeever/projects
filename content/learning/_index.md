These started as long conversations with AI. Whenever I wanted to get into a new field — electronics, digital logic, embedded development, programming the Atari 2600 — I'd spend hours asking questions and following threads wherever they went. Along the way I figured out that I learned it much better when I captured what I was learning and edited it into something organized, instead of letting it scroll away in a chat window. Shaping it into a reference is what helped me structure and organize this new content in my own mind as well. These reference "books" are what came out of that: written for me first, and organized so I (or you) can come back and look things up.
