title: "Projects"
---

There's nothing quite like using something you built yourself, bringing new life back to something broken, or learning things you never expected along the way. A lot of these projects are things I once assumed were beyond me. But with my Sweetie, advice from people who knew more than I did, and a little help from AI, they became possible.

What finally makes the difference is simple: patience, a bit of luck, and the willingness to start before you feel ready. Ignore the doubts. Don't wait for perfect. Just begin. The satisfaction lasts a lot longer than the struggle ever does.

If this site does anything, I hope it nudges you to take on that project you've been thinking about.
